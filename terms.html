Important: Vett Trade is a matching and marketplace platform. We do not carry out plumbing work ourselves. The independent plumbing professional is responsible for the plumbing service they agree to provide. Vett Trade may facilitate matching, job tracking, quote acceptance and, on eligible jobs, payment through VettPay.

1. Using Vett Trade

You may submit a plumbing request through Vett Trade without paying a customer lead fee. You must provide information that is accurate to the best of your knowledge so we can assess and match the request appropriately.

2. Matching and availability

We may use information such as your location, job type, urgency and trade coverage to match or rank suitable plumbing professionals. We do not guarantee that a trade will be available, respond within a particular time, accept a job or provide a quote.

3. Trade checks

Vett Trade may review information supplied by trades before activating their accounts, including identity, insurance and relevant registration information where applicable. These checks are designed to support platform quality and safety, but they are not a guarantee of workmanship, conduct, financial standing, availability or suitability for every job.

4. Your contract with the trade

The plumbing professional remains responsible for the plumbing work, attendance, workmanship, materials, guarantees and any other service commitments. Vett Trade is not the employer or subcontractor of the plumbing professional. Where a quote is issued through Vett Trade, the trade sets the labour, materials and other agreed charges and you decide whether to accept that quote.

5. Quotes and changes

For Vett Trade-managed jobs, a trade may issue an itemised quote through the platform. You should review the description and total before accepting it. If the scope or price changes materially, you should agree the change with the trade before the additional work is carried out and, where available, have the revised amount recorded through the platform.

6. VettPay

On eligible Vett Trade-managed jobs, payment may be requested through VettPay after the agreed work has been marked complete. The amount presented to you for payment is the amount of the accepted quote unless you have agreed a revised amount. Vett Trade does not add its trade-side completion fee on top of the customer’s accepted quote.

Payments are processed using third-party payment infrastructure. Payment availability may depend on the assigned trade having completed the required payout setup. Vett Trade may record payment status against the relevant quote and job.

7. Customer contact details

When your request is assigned and the relevant trade lead has been accepted and paid for, your contact details may be made available to that trade so they can contact you about the request. Your email address may also be used to send quote and payment links relating to your job. Please read our Privacy Notice for more information.

8. Job tracking

Vett Trade may show status information such as contacted, quoted, won, completed or paid. These statuses are operational updates and may depend on information entered by the trade or payment system. They do not amount to an independent inspection or certification of the work.

9. Refunds, disputes and chargebacks

If there is a problem with the plumbing work or amount charged, you should normally contact the trade and Vett Trade promptly. Where a payment was made through VettPay, Vett Trade may help review platform records and coordinate payment-related action where appropriate. Refunds, payment disputes and chargebacks may also be subject to applicable law and the rules of the payment provider.

10. Emergencies

Do not rely on Vett Trade where there is an immediate risk to life, serious injury, fire, gas escape or another emergency requiring an emergency service or utility provider. Contact the appropriate emergency service or utility provider directly.

11. Problems, complaints and disputes

If the issue concerns the plumbing work, you should normally raise it directly with the trade first. You may also contact Vett Trade through our contact and complaints page so we can record the issue and review conduct on the platform. We may ask both sides for information. Unless required by law or expressly agreed, Vett Trade does not become the provider of the underlying plumbing service.

12. Misuse

You must not submit knowingly false, abusive, fraudulent or duplicate requests, attempt to obtain restricted trade information without a genuine job request, or misuse the platform in a way that harms customers, trades or Vett Trade.

13. Liability

Nothing in these terms excludes liability where it would be unlawful to do so. Subject to that, Vett Trade is responsible for operating the platform with reasonable care but is not responsible for the plumbing professional’s independent acts, omissions, workmanship, materials or failure to attend.

14. Changes

We may update these terms as the service changes. The version published when you submit a request or accept a platform quote will apply to that platform interaction, subject to any rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
