Common urgent plumbing requests

  • Burst or split pipework
  • Major active water leaks
  • Overflowing toilets or cistern failures
  • Leaks affecting ceilings, floors or nearby electrics
  • Sudden plumbing failures causing significant water loss

If there is an immediate danger involving electricity, gas, fire or personal safety, use the appropriate emergency service or utility emergency line rather than waiting for a plumbing match.

What happens after you submit?

  1. Tell us what happened and enter the Petts Wood/BR5/BR6 location.
  2. Choose Same day or Emergency.
  3. Vett Trade checks suitable independent plumber coverage.
  4. If matched, the plumber contacts you about attendance, price and next steps.
