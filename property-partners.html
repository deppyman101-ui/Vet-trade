Partner compliance

Authorised instructions, digitally recorded.

Property Partner accounts electronically sign VettTrade’s Property Partner Agreement before activation. The authorised representative confirms their authority to act, agrees not to bypass council, housing-association, landlord, freeholder or managing-agent approvals, and automatically receives a signed PDF copy. VettTrade retains the matching signed record.
