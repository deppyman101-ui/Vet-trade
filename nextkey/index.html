Simple questions.

The three NewAddy routes have different models, so we keep the charges and responsibilities clear.

Is NewAddy free for renters?

Yes. Creating a renter search profile and sending room enquiries are free.

How does NewAddy make money?

Private-home landlords pay £79 only after a successful NewAddy introduction becomes the tenant. Room hosts can use a free listing or buy optional Featured and Fast-Track upgrades. House swaps are free.

Does NewAddy handle the room tenancy or lodger agreement?

No. NewAddy provides the listing and private introduction. The person offering the room remains responsible for having permission to offer it, legal checks, safety, agreements, rent and deposits.

Does NewAddy approve house swaps?

No. Swap listings are introductions only. The parties must obtain the required approval from their council, housing association or other landlord before any exchange.
