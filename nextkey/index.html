NextKey pilot terms summary: listing and receiving interest are free. If a renter first introduced through NextKey becomes the tenant for this property within 90 days, a £79 successful-introduction fee is payable by the landlord/property provider. The fee must not be passed to the tenant. The landlord remains responsible for the tenancy, legal checks, property compliance, deposit/rent arrangements and final applicant decision.
Evidence pack later: when there is a real match, NextKey can collect the applicant evidence the landlord needs and only share it after the renter authorises that application. This public form deliberately does not collect sensitive documents.
