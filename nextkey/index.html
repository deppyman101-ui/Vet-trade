NextKey pilot terms summary: listing and receiving interest are free. If a renter first introduced through NextKey becomes the tenant for this property within 90 days, a £79 successful-introduction fee is payable by the landlord/property provider. The fee must not be passed to the tenant. The landlord remains responsible for the tenancy, statutory checks, property compliance, deposit/rent arrangements and final applicant decision.

Simple questions.

Enough information to explain the pilot clearly without burying people in terms or documents.

Is NextKey free for renters?

Yes. Renters can join the pilot and receive matching contact for free.

Is it free for landlords to list?

Yes. There is no listing fee. Any successful-introduction charge is explained clearly in the landlord listing journey before a landlord submits a property.

Do renters upload documents straight away?

No. The public join form deliberately avoids collecting sensitive documents. Evidence is only requested later when there is a genuine property match and the renter chooses to progress.

What properties are you focused on?

The first pilot is focused mainly on studios and 1-bedroom private rentals across South-East London.
