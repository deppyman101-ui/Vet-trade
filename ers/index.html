How ERS helps

You do not have to manage every part yourself.

Executors and families can suddenly be left dealing with an entire property of possessions, different contractors, decisions about valuables and a property that still needs to be prepared for its next stage.

ERS becomes the practical coordinator. You remain in control; we organise the work.

01

Assess & record

We review the property and contents, record significant items and identify what may need specialist attention.

02

Value & sell

Where authorised, we coordinate independent valuers, auction houses, specialist buyers and suitable sale routes.

03

Donate & clear

We coordinate approved donation, recycling and clearance while protecting items that should not be disposed of.

04

Prepare & hand over

Cleaning, garden work, locksmiths, minor trades, final photographs and practical handover can all be coordinated.

The outcome

A property that is organised, documented and ready for what comes next.

  • Important and potentially valuable contents have been considered before clearance.
  • Approved sales, donation, recycling and contractor work are coordinated rather than left for you to chase.
  • You receive a clear record of the agreed work, costs and final handover.
  • The property can be left ready for sale, auction, transfer, rental or another agreed next stage.

ERS is not simply a clearance company. The aim is to manage the practical estate-property process properly from the first assessment to the final handover.

Common questions

Before you instruct ERS.

If you are not sure what service is needed, the assessment is designed to establish that.

Do I need Grant of Probate before contacting ERS?

No. You can contact ERS earlier. The authority required depends on the work requested, and substantive actions such as sale, donation or disposal require appropriate authority.

Will ERS just clear everything?

No. Potentially significant, sentimental or disputed items can be identified and held for a client decision before authorised clearance.

Does ERS provide formal valuations?

ERS identifies items that may warrant specialist attention and coordinates appropriate independent valuers, auction houses or specialist buyers where required.

Can a solicitor refer a client?

Yes. Solicitors and other appropriate professional referrers can introduce an executor or authorised representative directly to ERS.
