Customer protection through clarity

You pay the quote you accepted.

The trade creates an itemised quote showing labour, materials and any other agreed cost. Once you accept it and the agreed work is marked complete, a VettPay payment request can be issued.

No VettTrade completion fee is added to the customer’s accepted quote. Any applicable VettTrade platform fee is handled on the trade side.

VettPay is available on eligible VettTrade-managed jobs where the assigned trade has completed VettPay payout setup.

1

2

3

4

Customer pays securely

VettPay opens secure card checkout and records the payment against the quote and job.
