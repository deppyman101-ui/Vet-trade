Cancellation Policy

We aim to keep cancellation simple and fair. These terms do not remove any statutory cancellation rights you may have.

Before collection

You can cancel a booking before the garments are collected. We will not charge an automatic cancellation penalty. If you have already paid, we will refund the cancelled service unless a lawful, clearly explained deduction applies for direct costs already incurred.

If you ask us to start during the statutory cancellation period

Most Presse’d bookings are intended to be completed quickly. When booking online, we ask you to expressly request that we begin the service during the applicable cancellation period. If you cancel after the service has started, the law may allow us to charge a proportionate amount for the service already supplied. Once the service has been fully performed, your change-of-mind cancellation right may end where the legal requirements for that early start and acknowledgement have been met.

After collection

If you cancel after we have collected the garments but before pressing has started, contact us immediately. We will stop work where reasonably possible. We may recover reasonable direct costs actually incurred in collecting, handling or returning the garments where the law allows.

After pressing has started

If work has already begun at your express request, any refund may be reduced by a reasonable and proportionate amount for work already completed. We will explain any deduction.

If Presse’d cancels

If we cannot fulfil a confirmed booking, we will offer a reasonable alternative where possible. If that is not suitable, any amount paid for the affected service will be refunded.

Missed collection or return

If no one is available during the booked window, we will normally try to rearrange. Repeated or avoidable failed visits may result in us recovering reasonable direct costs where lawful, but we do not apply an automatic fixed penalty simply because a visit was missed.

How to cancel

Email pressedservice4u@gmail.com with your name, booking reference, postcode and a clear statement that you want to cancel. Once our dedicated business number and WhatsApp are live, those channels will also be available for cancellation requests.

Model cancellation wording

You may use: “I give notice that I cancel my Presse’d ironing service booking. Name: [name]. Booking reference: [reference]. Address/postcode: [address]. Date: [date].”
