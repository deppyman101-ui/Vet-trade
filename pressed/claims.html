Claims & Complaints Policy

If something goes wrong, tell us promptly so we can investigate and put it right fairly. Nothing here limits your statutory consumer rights.

Pressing quality

If you believe an item has not been pressed to a reasonable standard, contact us as soon as possible. Where appropriate we will offer a free re-press of the affected item rather than charging again.

Damage or loss

If an item is lost or appears to have been damaged while in our care, tell us promptly and provide the booking reference, description of the item, photographs where relevant and any proof of purchase or value you reasonably have. We will investigate the collection record, item count, garment condition, care instructions and handling history.

Pre-existing or inherent issues

We are not responsible for damage that was already present or that results from an inherent weakness, hidden defect or deterioration that could not reasonably have been identified. This does not excuse damage caused by a failure to use reasonable care and skill.

Care labels and special items

Please identify delicate, specialist, embellished, heat-sensitive or unusually valuable items before collection. We may refuse to press an item if we believe the process could damage it. If we agree a special handling method, we will follow that agreement with reasonable care.

Valuables and pockets

Please empty pockets before collection. Cash, jewellery, keys, electronics and other valuables should not be placed inside garments or bags sent for pressing. If we find an item, we will make reasonable efforts to return it.

How we resolve claims

Depending on the circumstances, a fair resolution may include re-performing the service, refunding all or part of the service charge, repair where appropriate, or reasonable compensation for proven loss or damage. We will consider the age, condition and value of the item and the circumstances of the claim. We do not apply an arbitrary compensation cap that overrides rights you have by law.

When to report

Please report issues as soon as reasonably possible, ideally within 48 hours of return so the facts are easier to verify. A delay in reporting does not by itself remove any statutory rights you may have.

Complaints

Email pressedservice4u@gmail.com with your name, booking reference and a short description of the issue. We will acknowledge the complaint and aim to resolve it reasonably and promptly.
