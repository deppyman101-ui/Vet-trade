Privacy Notice

This notice explains what personal information Presse’d by D&B collects, why we use it and the choices you have.

Who we are and how to contact us

Presse’d by D&B provides ironing collection and return services. For privacy questions, email pressedservice4u@gmail.com. Our dedicated business email, contact number and business correspondence address will be updated before public launch.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, collection and return address, postcode, access instructions, booking details, garment/service notes, account details, booking history, payment status, communications with us and basic technical/security information generated when you use the website. We do not store your account password in readable form and we do not intend to store full payment-card details ourselves.

Why we use your information

We use your information to create and secure your My Presse’d account, take and manage bookings, plan routes, collect and return garments, provide customer support, handle complaints or claims, take or reconcile payment, prevent misuse and keep records needed for tax, accounting or legal purposes.

Our lawful bases

We mainly process customer information because it is necessary to perform or take steps to enter into our service contract with you. We may also process information to meet legal obligations and for legitimate interests such as running the service safely, preventing fraud, maintaining records and improving operations where those interests do not override your rights. We will rely on consent where consent is the appropriate legal basis, for example for optional marketing.

Who we share information with

We use service providers to operate the website and customer accounts, including hosting, database/authentication, email/communications and payment providers. Our current website infrastructure includes Vercel for hosting and Supabase for database and authentication services. We only share information where needed for the service, legal compliance or professional advice, and we do not sell customer data.

International processing

Some technology providers may process information outside the UK. Where this happens, we expect appropriate contractual or legal safeguards to be used for the transfer.

How long we keep information

We keep active account and booking information for as long as needed to provide the service and manage the customer relationship. We may retain booking, payment and accounting records for up to six years where needed for tax, accounting, legal or dispute purposes. Information that is no longer needed will be deleted or anonymised where practical.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict your information, object to certain processing, and receive certain information in a portable form. You can contact us to exercise your rights. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office if you are unhappy with how your personal information is handled.

Cookies and browser storage

The current Presse’d website uses browser storage needed for account sessions, booking continuity and security. We are not currently using advertising cookies or optional marketing analytics on the Presse’d pages. If we add non-essential cookies or analytics later, we will update this notice and obtain consent where required.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the service, suppliers or law changes. The latest version and update date will be shown here.
