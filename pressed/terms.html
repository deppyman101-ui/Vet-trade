Time slots are arrival windows rather than guaranteed exact appointment times. Traffic, access and earlier collections can cause reasonable variation. Please make sure someone can provide or receive the garments, or that an alternative arrangement has been agreed in advance.

We normally follow the garment care label and use reasonable care and skill. We may decline an item where we believe pressing could cause damage. Please empty pockets before collection.
