PROPERTY AUTHORITY RULE
Do not start work at a council property, housing-association property, managed block or commercial property unless the VettTrade job record confirms authorised instruction from the responsible council, housing association, landlord, managing agent, property owner or authorised business representative. If authority is unclear, stop and contact VettTrade.
