Live call guide

Customer call script

1 · OPEN

“Hi, Vett Trade, [your name] speaking. How can I help?”

2 · EXPLAIN

“I can take a few details and see which available local plumbing professional is the best match for you.”

3 · TAKE DETAILS

Ask for their name, best contact number, area/postcode, what happened, whether damage is ongoing, urgency and property type.

4 · CONSENT

“Is it okay for Vett Trade to use these details to handle your request and share your contact details with the independent plumber we assign?”

5 · AFTER CREATE

“Perfect, I’ve got that set up. I’m sending you your tracking details now so you can follow the request.”

6 · CLOSE

“I’m checking the best available match now. Once a plumber takes the job, they’ll contact you directly about availability, the work and price.”

Important: Never promise that a plumber is definitely coming until one has accepted the job.
