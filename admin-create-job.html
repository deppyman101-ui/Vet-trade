Call guide

Keep it simple

1 · OPEN

“Hi, VettTrade, how can I help?”

2 · IDENTIFY THE TRADE

Work out whether this is plumbing, electrical, carpentry/handyman, locksmith or decorating.

3 · DETAILS

Take name, phone, email if possible, location, urgency, property type and a clear description.

4 · CONSENT

“Is it okay for VettTrade to use these details to handle the repair and share them with the assigned trade?”

5 · CLOSE

“I’m checking an approved matching trade now. I’ll send your tracking reference.”

Do not promise attendance until a trade has confirmed availability.
