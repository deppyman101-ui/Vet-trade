# Vett Trade

[Vett Trade](https://www.vetttrade.co.uk/) is a UK plumbing trade-matching platform that helps customers submit plumbing requests and connects suitable jobs with independent plumbing professionals covering selected areas of South East London and Kent.

## Current local coverage

Vett Trade currently targets plumbing enquiries in:

- Lewisham
- Catford
- Bromley
- Beckenham
- Orpington
- Bexley
- Chislehurst
- Sidcup

## How it works

Customers describe the plumbing problem, location and urgency. Vett Trade reviews the request and matches it against the coverage and work preferences of available independent plumbing professionals. Vett Trade is the matching platform; trades remain independent businesses responsible for discussing and carrying out the work.
