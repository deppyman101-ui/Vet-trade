Common urgent plumbing requests

  • Burst or split pipework
  • Major active water leaks
  • Overflowing toilets or cistern problems
  • Loss of water from a plumbing failure
  • Leaks threatening floors, ceilings or electrics

If there is immediate danger involving electricity, gas, fire or personal safety, use the appropriate emergency service or utility emergency line rather than waiting for a plumbing match.
